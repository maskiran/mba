## M1 Introduction to Managerial Accounting
* Managerial accounting focuses on providing information for internal decision-making.
* The key differences between financial and managerial accounting include users, purpose, and reporting requirements.
* Cost behavior analysis helps understand how costs change with activity levels.
* Variable costs change proportionally with activity, while fixed costs remain constant.
* Mixed costs contain both variable and fixed components.

## M2 Cost-Volume-Profit Analysis
* CVP analysis helps understand the relationship between costs, volume, and profit.
* The contribution margin is sales revenue minus variable costs.
* The break-even point is where total revenue equals total costs.
* Operating leverage measures the sensitivity of profit to changes in sales volume.
* Target profit analysis helps determine the sales volume needed to achieve desired profit.

## M3 Job Order Costing
* Job order costing is used when products are unique or made to order.
* Direct materials, direct labor, and manufacturing overhead are assigned to jobs.
* Predetermined overhead rates are used to apply overhead to jobs.
* Job cost sheets track all costs associated with specific jobs.
* Over- or under-applied overhead occurs when actual overhead differs from applied overhead.

## M4 Process Costing
* Process costing is used for mass production of identical units.
* Costs are accumulated by department or process rather than by job.
* Equivalent units of production measure work done on partially completed units.
* The weighted-average method combines beginning inventory with current period costs.
* The FIFO method separates beginning inventory costs from current period costs.

## M5 Activity-Based Costing
* ABC allocates overhead costs based on activities that drive costs.
* Cost drivers are factors that cause costs to be incurred.
* ABC provides more accurate product costs than traditional costing methods.
* Activity cost pools group similar activities together.
* ABC helps identify non-value-added activities and opportunities for cost reduction.

## M6 Budgeting
* The master budget includes operating and financial budgets.
* Sales budget is typically the starting point for the operating budget.
* Production budget is based on sales budget and desired ending inventory.
* Cash budget helps manage cash flows and identify financing needs.
* Flexible budgets adjust for actual activity levels.

## M7 Standard Costs and Variances
* Standard costs are predetermined costs for materials, labor, and overhead.
* Material variances include price and quantity variances.
* Labor variances include rate and efficiency variances.
* Overhead variances include spending and volume variances.
* Variance analysis helps identify areas needing attention.

## M8 Performance Measurement
* Balanced scorecard measures performance from multiple perspectives.
* Key performance indicators (KPIs) track critical success factors.
* Return on investment (ROI) measures profitability relative to investment.
* Residual income considers the cost of capital.
* Transfer pricing affects performance evaluation of divisions.
