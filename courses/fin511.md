## M1 Introduction to Investments
* Investments involve committing money to earn a return.
* Risk and return are fundamental concepts in investment analysis.
* Financial markets facilitate the trading of securities.
* Market efficiency affects investment strategies.
* Investment vehicles include stocks, bonds, and derivatives.

## M2 Risk and Return
* Expected return is the weighted average of possible returns.
* Risk is measured by standard deviation or variance.
* The risk-return tradeoff suggests higher risk requires higher expected return.
* Diversification reduces portfolio risk.
* The Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) relates risk to expected return.

## M3 Portfolio Theory
* Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT) focuses on optimal portfolio construction.
* The efficient frontier shows optimal risk-return combinations.
* The optimal portfolio depends on investor risk tolerance.
* Beta measures systematic risk relative to the market.
* The Sharpe ratio measures risk-adjusted return.

## M4 Stock Analysis
* Fundamental analysis examines company financials and operations.
* Technical analysis studies price patterns and market indicators.
* Valuation methods include DCF, P/E ratio, and book value.
* Market efficiency affects the effectiveness of analysis.
* Behavioral finance considers psychological factors in investing.

## M5 Bond Analysis
* Bonds are debt securities with fixed income.
* Bond prices and yields move inversely.
* Duration measures bond price sensitivity to interest rates.
* Credit risk affects bond yields.
* Yield curves show interest rates across maturities.

## M6 Derivatives
* Derivatives derive value from underlying assets.
* Options give the right to buy or sell at a specified price.
* Futures contracts obligate future delivery.
* Swaps exchange cash flows between parties.
* Hedging uses derivatives to reduce risk.

## M7 Alternative Investments
* Real estate investments include direct ownership and REITs.
* Private equity invests in non-public companies.
* Hedge funds use various strategies to generate returns.
* Commodities include physical goods and futures.
* Cryptocurrencies are digital assets with unique characteristics.

## M8 Investment Management
* Asset allocation determines portfolio composition.
* Rebalancing maintains target allocations.
* Active management tries to beat the market.
* Passive management tracks market indexes.
* Performance evaluation considers risk-adjusted returns.
