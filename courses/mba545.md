# Marketing in Our New Digital World – MBA 545

Coursera: [MOOC 1](https://www.coursera.org/learn/digital-marketing-revolution) [MOOC 2](https://www.coursera.org/learn/marketing-digital)

## M1 Digital Marketing Fundamentals
* Digital marketing uses electronic devices and the internet.
* Key digital marketing channels include social media, email, and search engines.
* The digital marketing mix includes owned, earned, and paid media.
* Digital marketing metrics track performance and ROI.
* Customer journey mapping helps understand digital touchpoints.

## M2 Social Media Marketing
* Social media platforms enable direct customer engagement.
* Content strategy drives social media success.
* Social listening monitors brand mentions and sentiment.
* Influencer marketing leverages trusted voices.
* Social media analytics measure engagement and reach.

## M3 Search Engine Marketing
* SEO improves organic search visibility.
* Keyword research identifies target search terms.
* On-page optimization includes content and technical elements.
* Off-page optimization builds authority through backlinks.
* PPC advertising targets specific keywords and audiences.

## M4 Content Marketing
* Content marketing creates valuable, relevant content.
* Content types include blogs, videos, and infographics.
* Content strategy aligns with business goals.
* Content distribution reaches target audiences.
* Content analytics measure engagement and conversion.

## M5 Email Marketing
* Email marketing nurtures customer relationships.
* List segmentation targets specific audiences.
* Email design affects open and click rates.
* A/B testing optimizes email performance.
* Email analytics track delivery and engagement.

## M6 Mobile Marketing
* Mobile marketing reaches users on smartphones.
* Mobile-friendly design is essential.
* Location-based marketing targets nearby customers.
* Mobile apps provide direct customer access.
* Mobile analytics track user behavior.

## M7 Digital Analytics
* Web analytics track website performance.
* Key metrics include traffic, conversion, and engagement.
* Attribution modeling assigns credit to touchpoints.
* A/B testing compares different versions.
* Data visualization communicates insights.

## M8 Digital Strategy
* Digital strategy aligns with business goals.
* Channel strategy selects appropriate platforms.
* Budget allocation optimizes spending.
* Performance measurement tracks success.
* Continuous optimization improves results.
